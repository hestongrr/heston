Selected project details

Representative projects

A closer look at a few projects that illustrate the range of work — from severe sulfate conditions and deep foundations to seismic site classification and environmental remediation.

Reverence 15 (77111-1) — Pulte Homes

Severe sulfate / Post-tensioned foundations

Geotechnical investigation revealing severe sulfate exposure conditions (highly corrosive to concrete). Recommended Type V cement with sulfate-resistant concrete mix design, post-tensioned foundation system, and Site Class C seismic designation. Included slope stability analysis for tiered pad layout.

Dove Pointe Place / Rainbow & Rancho II (26250-1) — Richmond American

21.7 acres / Uncontrolled fill / 8 foundation plan sets

Full geotechnical investigation for a 21.7-acre Richmond development. Up to 12 feet of uncontrolled fill removal required across the site. Low swell potential, S0 sulfate exposure. Eight separate foundation plan sets reviewed and stamped for construction.

John C. Fremont Middle School (32037-1) — CCSD

IBC 2018 / Full geotechnical report

Complete geotechnical investigation per IBC 2018 for the Clark County School District's Fremont Middle School replacement project. Included subsurface exploration, laboratory testing, seismic site classification, liquefaction assessment, settlement analysis, and foundation recommendations for the new school building.

US-93 & Golf Course Drive (44015-1) — Phase I ESA, Ely NV

Phase I Environmental Site Assessment for a commercial property at US-93 and Golf Course Drive in Ely, Nevada. Multiple revisions reflecting client and regulatory feedback. Representative of the rural Nevada ESA work covering White Pine County.

Data integrity

How these numbers were built

Every project count in this portfolio is backed by a specific data source — timesheets, report metadata, or project files — not by memory.

Timesheet records (2016–2022): 1,101 individual entries extracted from original .xls/.xlsx timesheet files, each showing year, project number, project name, and hours worked. The 335 unique project numbers come from this single source.

Report metadata: 11 geotechnical reports confirmed authored by Heston Norcott via DOCX author metadata and OLE document summary information from old .doc files. An additional 6 reports identified through OLE metadata extraction.

GeoTek phone backup: 156 files from a Google Drive phone backup containing 40+ unique GeoTek project numbers (2023–2025).

What's not included: GeoTek projects from 2023 (not backed up), some early Angle Engineering projects (2014–2015, paper records), and projects worked under other engineers' report numbers.
