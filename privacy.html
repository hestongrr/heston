Privacy

What this site collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted. Written against what the code actually does — not a template.

Last updated 3 August 2026 · thebigpicture.tech

The short version

This is a one-person engineering portfolio. I collect three things: anonymous usage statistics, standard web server logs, and whatever you type into the contact form. I don't sell anything to anybody, there are no advertising trackers, and no profile of you is built or shared.

The part worth calling out

Contact-form messages are forwarded through Discord, which means the contents of your enquiry pass through a third-party service, not just my server. If you'd rather that didn't happen, email me directly at heston.norcott@gmail.com instead of using the form. That reaches me the same way with one fewer party involved.

The tools themselves don't send your data anywhere

Every calculator, viewer, and 3D tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Soil data, borehole values, slope geometry, and any .ifc model you open in the BIM viewer are processed locally on your own machine. None of it is uploaded, stored, or seen by me. If you close the tab, it's gone.

That's a deliberate design decision, not an accident — engineering inputs are often confidential, and the safest way to handle a client's numbers is to never receive them.

Cookies

The only cookies set are Google Analytics ones, used to tell repeat visits from new ones. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can block them with your browser's settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on, and every tool on the site will keep working normally.

How long things are kept

Server logs rotate automatically and old ones are overwritten. Contact-form messages are kept as long as the conversation is useful. If you want yours deleted sooner, ask and I'll do it.

Your data, your call

Email heston.norcott@gmail.com and I will, without arguing about it: tell you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or send it to you. If you're in the UK, EU, or California, you have these rights by law; you have them here regardless of where you live.

Children

This site is aimed at practising engineers and students. It isn't directed at children and I don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If what the site does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves.
