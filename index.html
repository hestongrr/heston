Geotechnical · drafting

Boring Log Generator

Enter soil strata, SPT values, and groundwater; get a clean, professional USCS boring log with hatch patterns and an SPT plot, exportable to PNG. Live in the browser.

Open tool → Geophysics · from my thesis

Surface-Wave Cavity Detection

A 2-D elastic finite-difference seismic simulator — the core method of my MS thesis. Fire a shot, watch P, S, and Rayleigh waves scatter off a buried cavity, read the gather.

Open tool → 3D site modeling · real USGS data

Red Rock 3D Site Model

Real elevation data of Red Rock Canyon, Nevada, built into an interactive 3D site block — terrain surface, subsurface geology in the cut faces, and a graded building pad. Orbit it.

Open tool → Soil mechanics · interactive

SoilBox

A falling-sand physics toy running real soil behavior: sand piles at its angle of repose, cohesive mud stands steeper, water finds its level. With the mechanics explained.

Open tool → 3D granular physics · OSHA Subpart P

Sand3D: Angle of Repose

Dump sand in 3D and watch it find its own angle of repose — then see why that angle is OSHA's 1.5H:1V limit for Type C soil. Dial loose to dense, measure the slope, and catch the pile trending steeper than the code allows.

Open tool → 3D equipment · safe vs. test

Nuclear Density Gauge 3D

A working 3D model of a nuclear density gauge (Cs-137 + Am-241/Be). Drive the source rod into the fill, open the cutaway to see the shielded source and detectors, watch the count climb.

Open model → Field reporting · ASTM D6938

Density & Compaction Test Sheet

Turn nuclear gauge readings into a clean report sheet: wet density and moisture in, dry density, % compaction, and color-coded pass/fail out — printable or PNG.

Open tool → Open-source BIM · IFC in browser

IFC / BIM Viewer

An open-source building-model viewer built on web-ifc and three.js. Parse and render real IFC files with no proprietary software — a bundled geotech foundations model (mat footing, retaining wall, fill), section through any model, or open your own .ifc.

Open viewer → Interactive 3D game · earthworks

Grade It: Earthworks

An earthworks simulator you play: blade and haul a building pad to design elevation (watch the cut/fill volumes and your budget), roll the lift to 95% compaction, drive the nuclear gauge to certify it, then build the foundations and grade the whole subdivision.

Background

A builder, not just a user

I hold an M.S. in Civil & Environmental Engineering from UNLV, where my thesis applied finite-difference seismic modeling to detect shallow buried cavities from surface waves. Since then I've led field programs and lab testing on projects across Nevada — drilling crews, statewide exploration, materials testing to ICC / ACI / NAQTC standards.

Somewhere along the way I started writing my own software to fix the workflows the industry tools got wrong — and more recently, running a full applied-AI stack on my own hardware. That combination gives me a practitioner's view of where engineering and AI software actually deliver, and where it's hype.

A good engineer solves the problem. A good lead makes the next person able to solve it too. That's the standard I build to.
