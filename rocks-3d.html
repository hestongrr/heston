Podiform chromite

Igneous — cumulate (ophiolite)

Light olive to brownish black. Cumulate texture: anhedral chromite crystals in a serpentine matrix that weathers to moderate yellowish brown on uncut surfaces. Cut crystals show dull alteration rinds around submetallic cores, with the same alteration tracing the fractures. Chromite is the principal ore of chromium — the source of stainless steel.

About these scans

Real hand samples digitized with photogrammetry, pulled from the USGS 3D Science Catalog (3DSC) — Smithsonian 3D digitization hosted on USGS ScienceBase. The GLB files are loaded and rendered directly in the browser with three.js; no plugin, no server-side rendering.

USGS-authored products are in the public domain. Meshes were decimated and textures re-encoded for web delivery.
