About

Infinite slope — For c=0 fills, the critical failure is a plane parallel to the slope face. FS = tan φ / tan β. This is the lower bound for flat-crest cut slopes.

Planar wedge (Culmann) — Translational slide along a plane from the toe through the crest. Accounts for cohesion and crest width.

Bishop circular — Grid search for critical circles. For c=0 slopes with wide flat crests, net driving force (ΣW sin α) is typically ≤ 0, making circular surfaces non-critical. Still runs for c > 0 cases.

Water table — Pore pressure below the phreatic surface reduces effective normal stress (FS lower). Set zw below the slope base for dry conditions.
