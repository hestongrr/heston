3D site modeling · real USGS data

Red Rock 3D Site Model

Real elevation data of Red Rock Canyon, Nevada, built into an interactive 3D site block — terrain surface, subsurface geology in the cut faces, and a graded building pad. Orbit it.

Open tool → Soil mechanics · interactive

SoilBox

A falling-sand physics toy running real soil behavior: sand piles at its angle of repose, cohesive mud stands steeper, water finds its level. With the mechanics explained.

Open tool → 3D granular physics · OSHA Subpart P

Sand3D: Angle of Repose

Dump sand in 3D and watch it find its own angle of repose — then see why that angle is OSHA's 1.5H:1V limit for Type C soil. Dial loose to dense, measure the slope, and catch the pile trending steeper than the code allows.

Open tool → 3D equipment · safe vs. test

Nuclear Density Gauge 3D

A working 3D model of a nuclear density gauge (Cs-137 + Am-241/Be). Drive the source rod into the fill, open the cutaway to see the shielded source and detectors, watch the count climb.

Open model → 3D scans · real USGS specimens

Rocks 3D: Scanned Specimens

Photogrammetry scans of real rock hand samples from the USGS 3D Science Catalog — orbit a podiform chromite and a garnet-quartz-scheelite skarn, with the geology explained. Rendered live in the browser from GLB files.

Software & games

Tooling and simulation builds

Open-source software experiments and interactive simulators. The slope stability tool is a hand-rolled method-of-slices checker; the BIM viewer is built entirely on open-source IFC tooling.

Interactive 3D game · earthworks

Grade It: Earthworks

An earthworks simulator you play: blade and haul a building pad to design elevation (watch the cut/fill volumes and your budget), roll the lift to 95% compaction, drive the nuclear gauge to certify it, then build the foundations and grade the whole subdivision.

Play the game → Open-source BIM · IFC in browser

IFC / BIM Viewer

An open-source building-model viewer built on web-ifc and three.js. Parse and render real IFC files with no proprietary software — a bundled geotech foundations model (mat footing, retaining wall, fill), section through any model, or open your own .ifc.

Open viewer → Calculator · infinite slope & Bishop

Slope Stability Calculator

Three methods for flat-crest cut slopes — infinite slope, planar wedge (Culmann), and Bishop's simplified circular search — with water-table effects.
